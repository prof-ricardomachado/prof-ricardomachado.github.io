Permutação \(3\)-discordante \(\rightarrow \displaystyle\sum^n_{i=0}(-1)^i(n-i)!g(3,n,i)\text{,}\) no qual:
\begin{equation*} g(3,n,i)=\sum^{\lceil i/2\rceil}_{\alpha=0}\sum^{m}_{\beta=0}\frac{n}{n-i}2^{\beta}\binom{n+\alpha-i-1}{\alpha}\binom{n-i}{\beta}\binom{n-\alpha-1}{i-2\alpha-\beta}, \end{equation*}
para \(0\leq i\lt n\) e \(\displaystyle g(3,n,n)=3+\sum^{\lceil n/2\rceil}_{\alpha=1}\frac{n}{\alpha}\binom{n-\alpha-1}{\alpha-1}\text{,}\) com \(m=\min(n-i,i-2\alpha)\text{.}\)
