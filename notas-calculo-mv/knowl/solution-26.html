Solução 2.4.8.1.

Aqui, \(P(x,y) = 3y - e^{\sin x}\) e \(Q(x,y) = 7x + \sqrt{y^4+1}\text{.}\) Daí, \(\frac{\partial P}{\partial y} = 3\) e \(\frac{\partial Q}{\partial x} = 7\text{.}\)
Aplicando o Teorema de Green:
\begin{align*} \oint_{C} (3y - e^{\sin x}) \, dx + (7x + \sqrt{y^4+1}) \, dy \amp = \iint_{D} (7 - 3) \, dA \\ \amp = 4 \iint_{D} 1 \, dA \\ \amp = 4 A(D)\\ \amp = 4 \cdot \pi(3)^2 \\ \amp = 36\pi \end{align*}
