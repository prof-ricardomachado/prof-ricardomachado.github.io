Parametrizamos a superfície por \(\varphi(u,v) = (x(u,v), y(u,v), z(u,v))\) com vetor normal fundamental:
\begin{equation*} \frac{\partial \varphi}{\partial u} \times \frac{\partial \varphi}{\partial v} = \left(\frac{\partial y}{\partial u}\frac{\partial z}{\partial v} - \frac{\partial z}{\partial u}\frac{\partial y}{\partial v}\right)\vec{i} + \left(\frac{\partial z}{\partial u}\frac{\partial x}{\partial v} - \frac{\partial x}{\partial u}\frac{\partial z}{\partial v}\right)\vec{j} + \left(\frac{\partial x}{\partial u}\frac{\partial y}{\partial v} - \frac{\partial y}{\partial u}\frac{\partial x}{\partial v}\right)\vec{k} \end{equation*}
