Integrando sobre o quadrado \(D = [0,1] \times [0,1]\text{:}\)
\begin{align*} \iint_D (2x - 2y) \, dA \amp = \int_0^1 \int_0^1 (2x - 2y) \, dx \, dy\\ \amp = \int_0^1 \left[x^2 - 2xy\right]_{x=0}^{x=1} \, dy\\ \amp = \int_0^1 (1 - 2y) \, dy\\ \amp = \left[y - y^2\right]_0^1 = (1 - 1) - (0) = 0 \end{align*}
