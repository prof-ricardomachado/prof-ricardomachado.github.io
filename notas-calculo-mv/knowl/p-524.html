Para o rotacional:
\begin{align*} \text{rot}\,\vec{F} \amp = \left(\frac{\partial(z^2)}{\partial y} - \frac{\partial(\cos y)}{\partial z}\right)\vec{i} + \left(\frac{\partial(x\text{sen}\,y)}{\partial z} - \frac{\partial(z^2)}{\partial x}\right)\vec{j} + \left(\frac{\partial(\cos y)}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial(x\text{sen}\,y)}{\partial y}\right)\vec{k}\\ \amp = (0 - 0)\vec{i} + (0 - 0)\vec{j} + (0 - x\cos y)\vec{k}\\ \amp = -x\cos y\,\vec{k} \end{align*}
