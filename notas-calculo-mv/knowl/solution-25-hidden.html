Note que \(\gamma\) está orientada positivamente e \(\vec{F}(x,y) = (x^4, xy)\) é \(C^1\text{.}\) Vamos usar o Teorema de Green. Temos:
\begin{align*} P(x,y) = x^4 \amp \implies \frac{\partial P}{\partial y} = 0\\ Q(x,y) = xy \amp \implies \frac{\partial Q}{\partial x} = y \end{align*}
Assim:
\begin{equation*} \oint_{\gamma} x^4 \, dx + xy \, dy = \iint_{D} \left( \frac{\partial Q}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial P}{\partial y} \right) dA = \iint_{D} y \, dA \end{equation*}
A região é \(D = \{(x,y) \in \mathbb{R}^2 : 0 \le x \le 1 \text{ e } 0 \le y \le 1-x\}\text{.}\) Daí:
\begin{align*} \iint_{D} y \, dA \amp = \int_{0}^{1} \int_{0}^{1-x} y \, dy \, dx = \int_{0}^{1} \left.\frac{y^2}{2}\right|_{0}^{1-x} dx\\ \amp = \int_{0}^{1} \frac{(1-x)^2}{2} \, dx = \frac{1}{2} \int_{0}^{1} (1 - 2x + x^2) \, dx\\ \amp = \frac{1}{2} \left.\left( x - x^2 + \frac{x^3}{3} \right)\right|_{0}^{1} = \frac{1}{2} \left( 1 - 1 + \frac{1}{3} \right) = \frac{1}{6} \end{align*}
