Calculando o divergente do campo \(\vec{F} = x(x^2+y^2+z^2)\vec{i} + y(x^2+y^2+z^2)\vec{j} + z(x^2+y^2+z^2)\vec{k}\text{:}\)
\begin{align*} \text{div}\,\vec{F} \amp = \big[(x^2+y^2+z^2) + 2x^2\big] + \big[(x^2+y^2+z^2) + 2y^2\big] + \big[(x^2+y^2+z^2) + 2z^2\big]\\ \amp = 5(x^2 + y^2 + z^2) \end{align*}
Em coordenadas esféricas, o sólido \(E\) é dado por \(0 \le \rho \le 1\text{,}\) \(0 \le \varphi \le \frac{\pi}{4}\) e \(0 \le \theta \le 2\pi\text{,}\) com \(\text{div}\,\vec{F} = 5\rho^2\text{:}\)
\begin{align*} \iint_S \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{S} \amp = \int_0^{2\pi} \int_0^{\pi/4} \int_0^1 (5\rho^2) \rho^2 \text{sen}\,\varphi \, d\rho \, d\varphi \, d\theta\\ \amp = 5 (2\pi) \left[ -\cos\varphi \right]_0^{\pi/4} \left[ \frac{\rho^5}{5} \right]_0^1\\ \amp = 10\pi \left(1 - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right) \left(\frac{1}{5}\right) = \pi(2 - \sqrt{2}) \end{align*}
