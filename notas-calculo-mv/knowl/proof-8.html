Demonstração

Usando as definições de divergente e rotacional:
\begin{align*} \text{div}(\text{rot}\,\vec{F}) \amp = \nabla \cdot (\nabla \times \vec{F})\\ \amp = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(\frac{\partial R}{\partial y} - \frac{\partial Q}{\partial z}\right) + \frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial z} - \frac{\partial R}{\partial x}\right) + \frac{\partial}{\partial z}\left(\frac{\partial Q}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial P}{\partial y}\right)\\ \amp = \frac{\partial^2 R}{\partial x \partial y} - \frac{\partial^2 Q}{\partial x \partial z} + \frac{\partial^2 P}{\partial y \partial z} - \frac{\partial^2 R}{\partial y \partial x} + \frac{\partial^2 Q}{\partial z \partial x} - \frac{\partial^2 P}{\partial z \partial y}\\ \amp = 0 \end{align*}
pois todos os termos se cancelam aos pares pelo Teorema de Clairaut-Schwarz.
