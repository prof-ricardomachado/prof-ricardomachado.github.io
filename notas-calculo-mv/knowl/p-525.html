Usando a parametrização esférica com \(R=1\text{,}\) temos \(\|\alpha_\varphi \times \alpha_\theta\| = \sin\varphi\) e \(x = \sin\varphi\cos\theta\text{:}\)
\begin{align*} \iint_{S} x^2 \, dS \amp = \int_{0}^{2\pi} \int_{0}^{\pi} (\sin\varphi\cos\theta)^2 \sin\varphi \, d\varphi \, d\theta\\ \amp = \left(\int_{0}^{2\pi} \cos^2\theta \, d\theta\right) \left(\int_{0}^{\pi} \sin^3\varphi \, d\varphi\right) \end{align*}
