Como \(\int_{\gamma} \vec{F}(x,y) \cdot d\vec{r} = \int_{\gamma} P(x,y) \, dx + \int_{\gamma} Q(x,y) \, dy\text{,}\) segue que:
\begin{equation*} \int_{\gamma} \vec{F}(x,y) \cdot d\vec{r} = \iint_{D} \left( \frac{\partial Q}{\partial x}(x,y) - \frac{\partial P}{\partial y}(x,y) \right) dA \end{equation*}
