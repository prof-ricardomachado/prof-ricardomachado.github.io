Considere \(\vec{F}(x,y) = P(x,y)\vec{i} + Q(x,y)\vec{j}\) contínuo e \(C: [a,b] \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^2\) uma curva suave parametrizada por \(C(t) = (x(t), y(t))\text{.}\) Temos:
\begin{align*} \int_C \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{r} \amp = \int_{a}^{b} \vec{F}(C(t)) \cdot C'(t) \, dt\\ \amp = \int_{a}^{b} \vec{F}(x(t), y(t)) \cdot (x'(t), y'(t)) \, dt\\ \amp = \int_{a}^{b} [P(x(t),y(t))\vec{i} + Q(x(t),y(t))\vec{j}] \cdot [x'(t)\vec{i} + y'(t)\vec{j}] \, dt\\ \amp = \int_{a}^{b} (P(x(t),y(t))x'(t) + Q(x(t),y(t))y'(t)) \, dt \end{align*}
