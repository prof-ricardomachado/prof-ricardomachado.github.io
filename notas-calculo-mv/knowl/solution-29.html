Solução 2.3.4.2.1.

Item a) \(\frac{\partial P}{\partial y} = \sec^2 y = \frac{\partial Q}{\partial x}\) na faixa simplesmente conexa \(y \in (-\pi/2, \pi/2)\text{.}\) Potencial: \(f(x,y) = x\tan y\text{.}\)
\begin{equation*} \int_C \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{r} = f(2, \pi/4) - f(1,0) = 2\tan(\pi/4) - 0 = 2 \end{equation*}
Item b) \(\frac{\partial P}{\partial y} = -e^{-x} = \frac{\partial Q}{\partial x}\) em \(\mathbb{R}^2\text{.}\) Potencial: \(f(x,y) = x + y e^{-x}\text{.}\)
\begin{equation*} \int_C \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{r} = f(1,2) - f(0,1) = (1 + 2e^{-1}) - 1 = \frac{2}{e} \end{equation*}
