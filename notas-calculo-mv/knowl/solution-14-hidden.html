Temos \(x(t) = t\text{,}\) \(y(t) = t^2\) e \(z(t) = t^3\text{.}\) Derivando o vetor posição:
\begin{equation*} \gamma'(t) = (1, 2t, 3t^2) \end{equation*}
Substituindo as variáveis no campo \(\vec{F}\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} \vec{F}(\gamma(t)) = (t, (t^2)^2, -t(t^2)) = (t, t^4, -t^3) \end{equation*}
Calculando o produto interno:
\begin{equation*} \vec{F}(\gamma(t)) \cdot \gamma'(t) = t(1) + t^4(2t) + (-t^3)(3t^2) = t + 2t^5 - 3t^5 = t - t^5 \end{equation*}
Integrando no intervalo \([0,1]\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} \int_{\gamma} \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{r} = \int_{0}^{1} (t - t^5) \, dt = \left.\left( \frac{t^2}{2} - \frac{t^6}{6} \right)\right|_{0}^{1} = \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{6} = \frac{1}{3} \end{equation*}
