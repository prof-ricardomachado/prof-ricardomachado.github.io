2. Cálculo pela integral tripla do divergente:
\begin{equation*} \text{div}\,\vec{F} = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}(x) + \frac{\partial}{\partial y}(y) + \frac{\partial}{\partial z}(z) = 1 + 1 + 1 = 3 \end{equation*}
Integrando sobre o sólido \(B\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} \iiint_{B} \text{div}\,\vec{F} \, dV = 3 \iiint_{B} 1 \, dV = 3 \cdot \text{Vol}(B) = 3 \cdot \left(\frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot 1\right) = \frac{1}{2} \end{equation*}
Verificando a igualdade.
