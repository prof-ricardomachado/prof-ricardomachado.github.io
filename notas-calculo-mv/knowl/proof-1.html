Demonstração

Temos que \(\int_{\gamma} \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{r} = \int_{a}^{b} \vec{F}(\gamma(t)) \cdot \gamma'(t) \, dt\text{.}\)
Como \(\vec{F} = \nabla\varphi\text{,}\) segue que:
\begin{equation*} \vec{F}(\gamma(t)) = \left( \frac{\partial\varphi}{\partial x_1}(\gamma(t)), \frac{\partial\varphi}{\partial x_2}(\gamma(t)), \dots, \frac{\partial\varphi}{\partial x_n}(\gamma(t)) \right) \end{equation*}
Daí, efetuando o produto interno:
\begin{align*} \vec{F}(\gamma(t)) \cdot \gamma'(t) \amp = \frac{\partial\varphi}{\partial x_1}(\gamma(t))\frac{dx_1(t)}{dt} + \dots + \frac{\partial\varphi}{\partial x_n}(\gamma(t))\frac{dx_n(t)}{dt}\\ \amp = \frac{d}{dt}\big(\varphi(\gamma(t))\big) \quad \text{(pela Regra da Cadeia)} \end{align*}
Logo:
\begin{equation*} \int_{\gamma} \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{r} = \int_{a}^{b} \frac{d}{dt}\big(\varphi(\gamma(t))\big) \, dt = \varphi(\gamma(b)) - \varphi(\gamma(a)) = \varphi(B) - \varphi(A) \end{equation*}
