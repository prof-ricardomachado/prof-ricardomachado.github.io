Superfície lateral \(S_1\text{:}\) com parâmetros \(\theta\) e \(z\text{,}\) \(x = \cos\theta\text{,}\) \(y = \sin\theta\text{,}\) \(z = z\text{,}\) onde \(0 \le \theta \le 2\pi\) e \(0 \le z \le 1 + \cos\theta\text{.}\) O elemento de área é \(\|r_\theta \times r_z\| = 1\text{.}\)
\begin{align*} \iint_{S_1} z \, dS \amp = \int_{0}^{2\pi} \int_{0}^{1+\cos\theta} z \, dz \, d\theta = \frac{1}{2} \int_{0}^{2\pi} (1 + \cos\theta)^2 \, d\theta\\ \amp = \frac{1}{2} \int_{0}^{2\pi} \left(1 + 2\cos\theta + \frac{1+\cos 2\theta}{2}\right) d\theta = \frac{3\pi}{2} \end{align*}
