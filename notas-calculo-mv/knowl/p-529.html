Convertendo a integral dupla para coordenadas polares no disco unitário \(D\) (\(0 \le r \le 1\text{,}\) \(0 \le \theta \le 2\pi\)):
\begin{align*} \iint_D 3(x^2+y^2) \, dA \amp = \int_0^{2\pi} \int_0^1 3r^2 (r) \, dr \, d\theta\\ \amp = 2\pi \int_0^1 3r^3 \, dr = 2\pi \left[\frac{3r^4}{4}\right]_0^1\\ \amp = 2\pi \left(\frac{3}{4}\right) = \frac{3\pi}{2} \end{align*}
