Supondo \(\vec{F}\) de classe \(C^1\text{,}\) segue pelo Teorema das Derivadas Cruzadas (Clairaut-Schwarz) que \(\varphi\) é de classe \(C^2\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} \frac{\partial^2\varphi}{\partial y \partial x} = \frac{\partial P}{\partial y} \quad \text{e} \quad \frac{\partial^2\varphi}{\partial x \partial y} = \frac{\partial Q}{\partial x} \end{equation*}
Como \(\frac{\partial^2\varphi}{\partial y \partial x} = \frac{\partial^2\varphi}{\partial x \partial y}\text{,}\) concluímos que:
\begin{equation*} \frac{\partial P}{\partial y} = \frac{\partial Q}{\partial x} \end{equation*}
