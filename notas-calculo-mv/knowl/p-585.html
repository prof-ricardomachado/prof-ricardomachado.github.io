Avaliando o campo sobre \(C(t)\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} \vec{F}(C(t)) = (\sqrt{3}\cos t, \sqrt{3}\sin t, \sin t\cos t) \end{equation*}
Calculando o produto escalar:
\begin{align*} \vec{F}(C(t)) \cdot C'(t) \amp = -\sqrt{3}\sin t\cos t + \sqrt{3}\sin t\cos t + 0 = 0 \end{align*}
Portanto:
\begin{equation*} \iint_{S} \text{rot}\,\vec{F} \cdot d\vec{S} = \int_{0}^{2\pi} 0 \, dt = 0 \end{equation*}
