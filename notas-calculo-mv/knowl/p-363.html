Usando o Teorema Fundamental do Cálculo, segue que:
\begin{align*} \int_{m}^{n} [P(x, g_1(x)) - P(x, g_2(x))] \, dx \amp = \int_{m}^{n} \int_{g_2(x)}^{g_1(x)} \frac{\partial}{\partial y} P(x,y) \, dy \, dx\\ \amp = \int_{m}^{n} \int_{g_1(x)}^{g_2(x)} -\frac{\partial}{\partial y} P(x,y) \, dy \, dx \end{align*}
