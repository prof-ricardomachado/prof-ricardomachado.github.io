O divergente é calculado pela soma das derivadas parciais:
\begin{equation*} \text{div}\,\vec{F} = \frac{\partial P}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial Q}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial R}{\partial z} \end{equation*}
Calculando cada termo:
\begin{align*} \frac{\partial}{\partial x}(e^x \text{sen}\,y) \amp = e^x \text{sen}\,y\\ \frac{\partial}{\partial y}(e^y \text{sen}\,z) \amp = e^y \text{sen}\,z\\ \frac{\partial}{\partial z}(e^z \text{sen}\,x) \amp = e^z \text{sen}\,x \end{align*}
Somando os resultados, obtemos \(\text{div}\,\vec{F} = e^x \text{sen}\,y + e^y \text{sen}\,z + e^z \text{sen}\,x\text{.}\)
