Aplicando o Teorema Fundamental do Cálculo (ou a Regra de L'Hôpital / Teorema do Valor Médio para Integrais):
\begin{equation*} \lim_{h \to 0^+} \frac{\int_{0}^{h} f_i(\gamma(t)) \, dt}{h} = \lim_{h \to 0^+} f_i(\gamma(h)) = \lim_{h \to 0^+} f_i(X + h\vec{e}_i) = f_i(X) \end{equation*}
