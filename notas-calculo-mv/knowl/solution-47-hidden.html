A integral é calculada pela soma do fluxo nas 6 faces:
  • Face superior (\(z=1\text{,}\) \(\vec{n}_1 = \vec{k}\)): \(\vec{F} \cdot \vec{k} = 1 \implies \iint = 1\text{.}\)
  • Face direita (\(y=1\text{,}\) \(\vec{n}_2 = \vec{j}\)): \(\vec{F} \cdot \vec{j} = -1 \implies \iint = -1\text{.}\)
  • Face inferior (\(z=0\text{,}\) \(\vec{n}_3 = -\vec{k}\)): \(\vec{F} \cdot (-\vec{k}) = -1 \implies \iint = -1\text{.}\)
  • Face esquerda (\(y=0\text{,}\) \(\vec{n}_4 = -\vec{j}\)): \(\vec{F} \cdot (-\vec{j}) = 1 \implies \iint = 1\text{.}\)
  • Face posterior (\(x=0\text{,}\) \(\vec{n}_5 = -\vec{i}\)): \(\vec{F} \cdot (-\vec{i}) = -x^2 = 0 \implies \iint = 0\text{.}\)
  • Face frontal (\(x=1\text{,}\) \(\vec{n}_6 = \vec{i}\)): \(\vec{F} \cdot \vec{i} = x^2 = 1 \implies \iint = 1\text{.}\)
Somando os fluxos parciais:
\begin{equation*} \iint_{\sigma} \vec{F} \cdot \vec{n} \, dS = 1 - 1 - 1 + 1 + 0 + 1 = 1 \end{equation*}
