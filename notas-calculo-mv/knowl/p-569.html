Parágrafo
Seja \(C(t) = (u(t), v(t))\text{,}\) com \(a \le t \le b\text{,}\) uma parametrização da fronteira orientada positivamente de \(D\text{,}\) de modo que \(\partial S = \varphi(C(t))\text{.}\) Pela Regra da Cadeia:
\begin{align*} \oint_{\partial S} P \, dx \amp = \int_{a}^{b} P(\varphi(C(t))) \frac{d}{dt}(x(u(t),v(t))) \, dt\\ \amp = \int_{a}^{b} P(\varphi(C(t))) \left(\frac{\partial x}{\partial u}\frac{du}{dt} + \frac{\partial x}{\partial v}\frac{dv}{dt}\right) dt\\ \amp = \oint_{C} P(\varphi(u,v))\frac{\partial x}{\partial u} \, du + P(\varphi(u,v))\frac{\partial x}{\partial v} \, dv \end{align*}
