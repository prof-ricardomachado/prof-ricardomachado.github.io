Derivadas parciais:
\begin{align*} \frac{\partial \alpha}{\partial \varphi} \amp = (R\cos\varphi\cos\theta, R\cos\varphi\sin\theta, -R\sin\varphi)\\ \frac{\partial \alpha}{\partial \theta} \amp = (-R\sin\varphi\sin\theta, R\sin\varphi\cos\theta, 0) \end{align*}
O produto vetorial resulta em:
\begin{equation*} \frac{\partial \alpha}{\partial \varphi} \times \frac{\partial \alpha}{\partial \theta} = R^2\sin^2\varphi\cos\theta\vec{i} + R^2\sin^2\varphi\sin\theta\vec{j} + R^2\sin\varphi\cos\varphi\vec{k} \end{equation*}
