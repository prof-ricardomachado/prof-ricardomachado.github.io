Solução 3.1.3.1.

Identificando as funções componentes:
\begin{equation*} P(x,y,z) = xyz, \quad Q(x,y,z) = x^2, \quad R(x,y,z) = 1 \end{equation*}
Aplicando a definição:
\begin{align*} \text{rot}\,\vec{F} \amp = \left(\frac{\partial}{\partial y}(1) - \frac{\partial}{\partial z}(x^2)\right)\vec{i} + \left(\frac{\partial}{\partial z}(xyz) - \frac{\partial}{\partial x}(1)\right)\vec{j} + \left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(x^2) - \frac{\partial}{\partial y}(xyz)\right)\vec{k}\\ \amp = (0 - 0)\vec{i} + (xy - 0)\vec{j} + (2x - xz)\vec{k}\\ \amp = xy\vec{j} + x(2-z)\vec{k} \end{align*}
