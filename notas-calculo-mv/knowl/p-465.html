Derivando em relação a \(y\) e comparando com ():
\begin{equation*} \frac{\partial\varphi}{\partial y} = ze^x\cos(yz) + \frac{\partial C}{\partial y}(y,z) = ze^x\cos(yz) \implies \frac{\partial C}{\partial y}(y,z) = 0 \end{equation*}
Logo, a constante de integração depende apenas de \(z\text{:}\) \(C(y,z) = C(z)\text{.}\) Assim:
\begin{equation*} \varphi(x,y,z) = e^x\text{sen}(yz) + C(z) \end{equation*}
